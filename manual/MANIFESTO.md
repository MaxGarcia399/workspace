# The Workspace manifesto

<!-- DRAFT — pending approval by the product owner. Not final copy. -->

Ten lines to decide what belongs in Workspace and what doesn't.
*[Léelo en español](MANIFIESTO.md)*

1. **Transparency, all the way down.** You see what each piece does, why, what it changes and what it costs.
2. **Useful before impressive.** If it doesn't save you real work, it doesn't ship.
3. **Simple by default.** Few pieces, done well; the advanced stuff waits until you ask for it.
4. **The person before the company.** If something is good for the product but bad for you, you win.
5. **Your files are yours.** Your agents and their memory live in readable folders you keep and can take with you.
6. **You choose the engine.** An agent isn't married to one vendor; switching engines doesn't mean starting over.
7. **Honest about limits.** We say what works, where, and with what — and what doesn't, too.
8. **Never a big change without asking.** Anything that touches your data or how you work is offered, not imposed.
9. **Fail without breaking.** A piece that fails tells you and steps aside; your session goes on.
10. **Taste in the details.** A tool you use every day deserves to look and feel good.
