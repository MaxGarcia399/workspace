# v1.0
stage: development
author: The Workspace team
updated: 2026-11-23

Workspace 1.0 brings the **Companion**: your agent lives in a corner of your screen. It chats with you, opens what you need and, before anything sensitive, asks your permission with a card. What already works stays: your agents, their brains, the 9 engines, the terminal and the page.

## What's coming
- [x] **Workspace Companion on Mac** — A bubble next to your agent, a big chat, offline voice dictation, opening apps and files, and email drafts that never send themselves.
- [x] **Cards next to the Companion** — Whatever needs your permission shows up beside it, colored by how sensitive it is, and waits for you even if you step away.
- [ ] **Its own cursor** — It presses buttons and scrolls pages without moving your mouse, in two modes: «Quiet» (does it behind the scenes) and «Watch» (you see its cursor go to the button).
- [ ] **Companion on Windows** — The same bubble, nothing to install.
